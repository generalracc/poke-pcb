See where every part goes on your JLCPCB board, then tick them off as you place them.

Drop your JLC files here

The gerber zip you uploaded, or the production zip JLC sends back. Add your BOM and pick-and-place (CPL) file too, as CSV or XLSX.

      Everything runs in this browser tab. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
      Which files work?
      • Gerbers as a zip, a folder or loose files, from KiCad, EasyEDA, Altium, Eagle or Fusion 360. Zips inside zips are fine.
      • KiCad gerbers usually say which pad belongs to which part. That gives exact highlights, pin 1 and net names without a CPL file.
      • Other gerbers need the CPL (pick-and-place) file to find parts. Positions are matched to the paste pads automatically.
      • BOM as CSV or XLSX with a designator column, plus value, footprint and LCSC columns if you have them. JLC's BOM template works as is.
      • PDF BOMs and old .xls files can't be read. Export them as CSV or XLSX.
